Welcome to the beautiful islands of Fiji

Fiji is an island nation in the South Pacific. Things to do and see in Fiji include warm weather, tropical waters, friendly residents, and other island experiences. Attractions and activities available to visitors to Fiji include beaches, botanical gardens, natural pools, resorts, and cultural experiences.

Explore Places Around Nadi

Here are 5 places you can visit while you're in Fiji:

  • Port Denarau - A marina with shops, restaurants, and boat trips.
  • Wailoaloa Beach - A relaxing beach near Nadi.
  • Garden of the Sleeping Giant - A tropical garden with beautiful plants.
  • Sabeto Mud Pools - A natural mud pool experience.
  • Sabeto Hot Springs - Warm geothermal pools surrounded by nature.

Why Visit Fiji?

Fiji offers both adventure and relaxation. Other attractions and activities in Fiji allow visitors to experience Fijian food and other elements of Fijian hospitality. Short- and long-term visitors alike have many choices.
