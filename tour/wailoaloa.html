Wailoaloa Beach: Relax by the Ocean

Wailoaloa Beach is located near Nadi and is a popular place for visitors who want to enjoy the ocean and watch the sunset. The area has accommodation, hotels, restaurants, and beachside places to relax. It is a good destination for travelers who want to stay near the beach while remaining close to Nadi town and the airport.

Things to Do

  • Walk along the beach.
  • Watch the sun set over the ocean.
  • Enjoy food at nearby restaurants.
  • Stay at a hotel or beachside accommodation.
