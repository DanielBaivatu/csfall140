Sabeto Mud Pools: A Natural Experience

Sabeto Mud Pools are located near Nadi in the Sabeto area. They offer visitors a different kind of tourist experience, where people can cover themselves in natural mud and wash it off afterward. This attraction is surrounded by Fiji's tropical landscape and is often visited together with other nearby attractions.

Things to Know

  • Follow the attraction's safety instructions.
  • Bring a change of clothes and a towel.
  • Check opening hours before visiting.
  • Enjoy the surrounding tropical scenery.
