Overview

The North Shore is a great place for swimming and surfing, with white beaches and blue seas. Conditions may change daily. You might be able to swim one day and watch surfers the next. This page provides information about some of the major North Shore sites and attractions.

Sunset Beach

Sunset Beach provides views of the shore and ocean at night. High waves in winter because of storms. Other seasons are suitable for other shoreline-related activities.

Banzai Pipeline (Ehukai)

Pipline is one of the most famous surf breaks in the world. The reef break produces fast, hollow waves. Pipline is a good place to watch experienced surfers from shore.

Waimea Bay

Waimea Bay is a wide, popular beach facing a bay with deep blue water. Swimming and snorkeling are popular summer activities. In winter the waves can get powerful, with world-famous surfing.
