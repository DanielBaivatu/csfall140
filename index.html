About World War II

World War II was a major global conflict that lasted from 1939 to 1945. Many countries around the world were involved in the war. The war had a major impact on history and changed many countries.

World War II History

World War II included many important battles and events. Some of the most well-known events include the attack on Pearl Harbor, D-Day, and the end of the war in Europe and Japan.
