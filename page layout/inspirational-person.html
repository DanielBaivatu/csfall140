Elder Jeffrey R. Holland

A Life Of Faith, Love. and Testimony

Elder Jeffrey-R-Holland was a leader in The Chruch of Jeus Christ of latter-day Saints who dedicate his life to serving Jesus Christ, setting an example for others and helping others grow their religious beliefs. Before becoming an Apostel, he worked in education and served as president of Brigham Young University. He encouraged others to have faith in the Savoir and be optimistic during difficult times. His life is an example of faith and dedication and love for the gospel.

One thing I like about Elder Jeffrey-R-Holland is his devotion he was when discussing repentance. He emphasizes the Lord's willingness to accept you with open arms. Most of my own conversion to the gospel is because of Elder Holland. His messages at the general conference and other YSA confrences. Every time I hear him speak, I am reminded that he is specifically addressing me. I love Elder Holland. Every time I have heard him speak, I have felt that he is speaking directly to me at a point in my life when I needed to hear it. Elder Holland has been very helpful to me and I thank him and others like him who serve by expressing their love for the Savior. I will always remember Elder Holland as someone who helped me draw closer to the Savior and feel His love

his Final General confrence Talk

"And Now I See

When Elder Holland gave a talk in a general conference session on October 5, 2025, the title of his talk was "And Now I See." In this talk, Elder Holland described a biblical story about a man born blind who was healed by Jesus and drew parallels between this story and the ways in which we can recognize God's work in our lives. Elder Holland also described the Book of Mormon's influence on his life.

"it has been, for me, a rod of safety for my soul." - Elder Jeffrey-R-Holland, "And Now I See"

This message helps me remember that scriptures can help us through trials. We may not fully understand our trials, but we can find comfort in our faith in Jesus Christ and His teachings. I will be encouraged by Elder Holland's talk to read the Book of Mormon and build faith in the Savior.

Why He Inspires Me

Elder Holland is a role model for me because he continued to share his testimony to others about Jesus Christ. His final General Conference message reminds me that faith can grow through personal experiences and scripture study. I hope to follow his example by strengthening my own testimony and daily testifying to others about the Savior.
